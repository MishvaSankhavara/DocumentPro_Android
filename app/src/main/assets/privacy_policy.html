Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: August 19, 2026

Welcome to Document Reader: Word, PDF, XLS ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us"). We are strongly committed to protecting your personal privacy and providing a secure, transparent user experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how our Application collects, uses, handles, and protects your information in compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

🔒 Core Privacy Guarantee: Your documents, files, and images are processed 100% locally on your device. We do NOT upload, read, store, or transmit the contents of your files to any external server or cloud service.

1. Information We Process & Collect

A. Document & File Content (On-Device Only)

When you view, edit, convert, compress, merge, split, or password-protect files (such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, CSV, TXT, or images), all processing is performed strictly within your device's local memory. None of your document data is sent to our servers or accessible to us.

B. Non-Personal & Technical Data

To ensure application stability, diagnose crashes, and provide features, our third-party analytics and ad service providers may automatically collect non-personally identifiable technical information, including:

  • Device model, manufacturer, hardware specifications, and OS version
  • Anonymous Advertising ID (Google GAID)
  • Language preferences and country-level locale
  • Application performance diagnostics, session duration, and crash trace logs

2. Permissions Requested & Why

The Application requests only the minimal set of permissions strictly necessary to deliver its core features:

Permission Target OS Purpose & Necessity
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Android 13+ (API 33+) Required only when you choose to use the Photo-to-PDF tool to convert your selected images into a PDF document.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Android 6.0 – 12 Required to scan and display your locally stored Word, PDF, Excel, and PPT files in the document reader.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Android 6.0 – 9.0 Required on legacy Android versions to save edited, merged, compressed, or converted document outputs.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Android 13+ (API 33+) Used to send document processing completion alerts and daily reading productivity reminders. (You can turn this off anytime in Settings).
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE All Android Versions Required to fetch remote configuration switches, send anonymous crash logs, and load advertisements via Google AdMob.

    Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    Ad Technology Providers: Google AdMob Partners

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Used to monitor application performance and diagnose crashes to improve app quality.
    Firebase Privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Our Application is designed for general audiences and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete such data.

5. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data. Because all document operations are conducted locally on your device, your sensitive files are never exposed to external networks. For diagnostic and network communications, we implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS / TLS).

6. Your Rights & Opt-Out Choices

You have full control over your privacy choices on your Android device:

  • App Permissions: You can revoke storage, image, or notification permissions at any time via your device's Settings > Apps > Document Reader > Permissions.
  • Personalized Ads Opt-Out: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time via Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.
  • Data Deletion Requests: You may contact us at any time to request clarification or deletion of any support correspondence.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
